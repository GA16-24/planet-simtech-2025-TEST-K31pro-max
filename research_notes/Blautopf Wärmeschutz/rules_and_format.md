# PlaNeT SimTech rules and D4RT layout

All URLs below were accessed on 2026-11-07. PDF page numbers in links refer to the physical PDF pages. D4RT measurements were taken directly from its PDF text objects with Poppler, converted to PostScript points at 72 pt per inch, and rounded where extraction introduced small errors. Millimetre conversions use 1 inch = 25.4 mm.

Scope control: no previous PlaNeT submission, winning report, solution summary, review, presentation, video, discussion, archive page, or existing solution repository was opened or used. The official homepage exposes an archive link and the FAQ mentions that archive, but neither was followed. D4RT was used only as a visual and typographic reference, not as scientific evidence.

## 1. What the Stand 10/2026 rules require

### Takeaway

The report must be an anonymous German A4 PDF in 12 pt type with at least 2.5 cm margins on every side. The main report has a 10-page maximum, the cover is separate, and bibliography plus appendix share a separate five-page allowance. One correctly named PDF must reach the organizer by the communicated Stuttgart deadline. AI is permitted, but its use must follow the input, output-checking, and disclosure rules. [Report guidelines, Stand 10/2026](https://www.planet-simtech.de/wp-content/uploads/2023/05/PlaNeT_Vorgaben-Bericht.pdf); [Participation conditions, Stand 10/2026](https://www.planet-simtech.de/wp-content/uploads/2023/05/PlaNeT_Teilnahmebedingungen.pdf)

### Cited Findings

#### Source status and language

- The retrieved report guidelines are six pages and carry "Stand: 10/2026" in the footer. The retrieved participation conditions are four pages and carry the same version date. These are the versions linked by the current official homepage. [Report guidelines](https://www.planet-simtech.de/wp-content/uploads/2023/05/PlaNeT_Vorgaben-Bericht.pdf); [Participation conditions](https://www.planet-simtech.de/wp-content/uploads/2023/05/PlaNeT_Teilnahmebedingungen.pdf); [Official homepage](https://www.planet-simtech.de/)
- The competition language is German. [Participation conditions, section 2, p. 1](https://www.planet-simtech.de/wp-content/uploads/2023/05/PlaNeT_Teilnahmebedingungen.pdf#page=1)
- The organizer provides no report template. Teams may choose their word-processing software, but must follow the formatting and content rules. [Official FAQ, "Fragen zum Bericht"](https://www.planet-simtech.de/faq/)

#### Page budget and required structure

- The main report may contain at most 10 pages. The cover, bibliography, and appendix do not count toward those 10 pages. Bibliography and appendix together may contain at most five pages. [Report guidelines, sections 1 and 2, pp. 1-2](https://www.planet-simtech.de/wp-content/uploads/2023/05/PlaNeT_Vorgaben-Bericht.pdf#page=2)
- The summary is part of the 10-page report and may not exceed one page. It must communicate the results and methods briefly, without technical jargon, so that an outsider can understand them. [Report guidelines, section 1, p. 1](https://www.planet-simtech.de/wp-content/uploads/2023/05/PlaNeT_Vorgaben-Bericht.pdf#page=1)
- Every report must contain a cover, summary, introduction, main part, and conclusion. The main part must present methods, results, and discussion. It must include mathematical methods, simulation tools, model assumptions, and general premises, followed by critical discussion of results in context. [Report guidelines, section 1, p. 1](https://www.planet-simtech.de/wp-content/uploads/2023/05/PlaNeT_Vorgaben-Bericht.pdf#page=1)
- The introduction must describe the problem and engage the reader. The conclusion must give a concise account of the most important results and briefly state what could be improved with more working time. [Report guidelines, section 1, p. 1](https://www.planet-simtech.de/wp-content/uploads/2023/05/PlaNeT_Vorgaben-Bericht.pdf#page=1)
- The bibliography and appendix are optional. The appendix may contain code developed during the competition. It is not a fixed part of the report and will not necessarily be considered in judging. [Report guidelines, section 1, p. 2](https://www.planet-simtech.de/wp-content/uploads/2023/05/PlaNeT_Vorgaben-Bericht.pdf#page=2)
- Separate supplementary files are not accepted. The FAQ explicitly says that the complete result must be contained in the report, including any program or spreadsheet work. This makes the permitted in-report appendix the only rule-supported place for code. [Official FAQ, "Fragen zum Bericht"](https://www.planet-simtech.de/faq/); [Report guidelines, section 1, p. 2](https://www.planet-simtech.de/wp-content/uploads/2023/05/PlaNeT_Vorgaben-Bericht.pdf#page=2)
- Partial solutions are permitted. The jury evaluates modelling and especially the derivation of new approaches, not only results from an earlier analysis. Assumptions must be made and discussed when the task does not supply data. [Report guidelines, sections 5 and 6, p. 3](https://www.planet-simtech.de/wp-content/uploads/2023/05/PlaNeT_Vorgaben-Bericht.pdf#page=3)

#### Cover and anonymity

- The cover is mandatory and does not count toward the 10-page limit. It must show the Team-ID, date, task statement, and competition name "PlaNeT SimTech". [Report guidelines, section 1, p. 1](https://www.planet-simtech.de/wp-content/uploads/2023/05/PlaNeT_Vorgaben-Bericht.pdf#page=1)
- The report guidelines prohibit participant and school names on the cover. The participation conditions are broader: neither the cover nor anywhere in the report may contain names or school information. Use the stricter whole-document rule. [Report guidelines, section 1, p. 1](https://www.planet-simtech.de/wp-content/uploads/2023/05/PlaNeT_Vorgaben-Bericht.pdf#page=1); [Participation conditions, section 10, pp. 2-3](https://www.planet-simtech.de/wp-content/uploads/2023/05/PlaNeT_Teilnahmebedingungen.pdf#page=3)
- The Team-ID supports anonymous jury review and serves as both the cover identifier and the filename. [Official FAQ, "Fragen zur Team-Logistik"](https://www.planet-simtech.de/faq/)

#### Typography and page geometry

- The paper format is A4. Every margin must be at least 2.5 cm, not merely the side margins. [Report guidelines, section 2, p. 2](https://www.planet-simtech.de/wp-content/uploads/2023/05/PlaNeT_Vorgaben-Bericht.pdf#page=2)
- The permitted fonts are Times New Roman, Arial, or a comparable font. The stated font size is 12 pt. Line spacing must be at least 1.0. [Report guidelines, section 2, p. 2](https://www.planet-simtech.de/wp-content/uploads/2023/05/PlaNeT_Vorgaben-Bericht.pdf#page=2)
- The submission must be a PDF. Failure to follow formal requirements can lead to a lower jury assessment. [Report guidelines, section 2, p. 2](https://www.planet-simtech.de/wp-content/uploads/2023/05/PlaNeT_Vorgaben-Bericht.pdf#page=2); [Participation conditions, section 12, p. 3](https://www.planet-simtech.de/wp-content/uploads/2023/05/PlaNeT_Teilnahmebedingungen.pdf#page=3)

#### References and independent work

- Teams may use freely accessible material, including internet sources and schoolbooks, if the jury can access it and the team cites it correctly. Third-party knowledge must be marked in the text and included in the bibliography. [Report guidelines, sections 1 and 3, pp. 2-3](https://www.planet-simtech.de/wp-content/uploads/2023/05/PlaNeT_Vorgaben-Bericht.pdf#page=2)
- A source is cited directly in the text with a number, as in `[1]`. The official internet-reference pattern is author or institution, title, URL, and access date. Verbatim quotations must be enclosed in quotation marks. [Report guidelines, section 3, pp. 2-3](https://www.planet-simtech.de/wp-content/uploads/2023/05/PlaNeT_Vorgaben-Bericht.pdf#page=2)
- All sources must be identified, and images must carry source credits. [Participation conditions, section 14, p. 3](https://www.planet-simtech.de/wp-content/uploads/2023/05/PlaNeT_Teilnahmebedingungen.pdf#page=3)
- The work must be independent. Only registered team members may contribute, and outside help leads to disqualification. The report guidelines prohibit contact with people outside the team by telephone, email, or public forums. The FAQ distinguishes reading a public forum, which is allowed with citation, from posting a question and using the answer, which is prohibited. [Report guidelines, section 3, p. 3](https://www.planet-simtech.de/wp-content/uploads/2023/05/PlaNeT_Vorgaben-Bericht.pdf#page=3); [Participation conditions, section 14, p. 3](https://www.planet-simtech.de/wp-content/uploads/2023/05/PlaNeT_Teilnahmebedingungen.pdf#page=3); [Official FAQ, "Fragen zum PlaNeT Day"](https://www.planet-simtech.de/faq/)
- A supervising teacher may help with preparation but may not help answer the task on the competition day. [Participation conditions, section 8, p. 2](https://www.planet-simtech.de/wp-content/uploads/2023/05/PlaNeT_Teilnahmebedingungen.pdf#page=2)

#### Filename, deadline, and submission channel

- Submit one PDF whose filename contains only the Team-ID, for example `1234.pdf`. The Team Captain is responsible for submitting the written solution. [Report guidelines, section 4, p. 3](https://www.planet-simtech.de/wp-content/uploads/2023/05/PlaNeT_Vorgaben-Bericht.pdf#page=3); [Participation conditions, sections 7.1 and 10, p. 2](https://www.planet-simtech.de/wp-content/uploads/2023/05/PlaNeT_Teilnahmebedingungen.pdf#page=2)
- The current report guidelines instruct teams to email the PDF to `planet@simtech.uni-stuttgart.de` by the fixed time on competition day. Receipt is confirmed by email. [Report guidelines, section 4, p. 3](https://www.planet-simtech.de/wp-content/uploads/2023/05/PlaNeT_Vorgaben-Bericht.pdf#page=3)
- The participation conditions say that the exact submission method, such as email or upload, will be announced beforehand. The current FAQ says the pre-event email will contain submission options and that email to the same address is generally possible. Check that email rather than assuming an upload channel. [Participation conditions, section 10, p. 2](https://www.planet-simtech.de/wp-content/uploads/2023/05/PlaNeT_Teilnahmebedingungen.pdf#page=2); [Official FAQ, "Fragen zum PlaNeT Day"](https://www.planet-simtech.de/faq/)
- The official deadline uses Stuttgart local time and is communicated by email. Any postponement will also be emailed. Late submissions are not possible. [Participation conditions, section 11, p. 3](https://www.planet-simtech.de/wp-content/uploads/2023/05/PlaNeT_Teilnahmebedingungen.pdf#page=3)
- For the current cycle, the FAQ gives the normal working window as 09:00 to 16:00 German time. The homepage lists PlaNeT Day on 28.11.2026, a 09:00 task release, and seven hours of work. The emailed deadline remains the controlling operational detail if the organizer changes it. [Official FAQ, "Fragen zum PlaNeT Day"](https://www.planet-simtech.de/faq/); [Official homepage](https://www.planet-simtech.de/); [Participation conditions, section 11, p. 3](https://www.planet-simtech.de/wp-content/uploads/2023/05/PlaNeT_Teilnahmebedingungen.pdf#page=3)
- Technical submission problems should be reported immediately by email or through the telephone number supplied with the competition information. [Report guidelines, section 8, p. 4](https://www.planet-simtech.de/wp-content/uploads/2023/05/PlaNeT_Vorgaben-Bericht.pdf#page=4); [Official FAQ, "Fragen zum PlaNeT Day"](https://www.planet-simtech.de/faq/)

#### AI use and disclosure

- AI tools such as ChatGPT, Copilot, and Grammarly are generally allowed. AI should support personal engagement, independent thinking, and scientific competence, not replace them. [Report guidelines, section 9, p. 4](https://www.planet-simtech.de/wp-content/uploads/2023/05/PlaNeT_Vorgaben-Bericht.pdf#page=4)
- Users are responsible for prompts and inputs. Do not enter copyrighted material into systems that store it or use it for training. Do not enter confidential or personal data. The organizer explicitly permits uploading the competition question itself. [Report guidelines, sections 9 and 10, pp. 4-5](https://www.planet-simtech.de/wp-content/uploads/2023/05/PlaNeT_Vorgaben-Bericht.pdf#page=4)
- Users are responsible for generated output. Before use, check correctness, possible similarity to existing works, and legal and ethical compatibility. AI-generated data are not self-validating. The official positive example requires an independent source that confirms the plausibility of initial generated test data. [Report guidelines, sections 9 and 10, pp. 4 and 6](https://www.planet-simtech.de/wp-content/uploads/2023/05/PlaNeT_Vorgaben-Bericht.pdf#page=6)
- Disclosure must state clearly and understandably where, how, and which AI system was used. Submission includes a declaration that the work follows the four AI principles. Misconduct can lead to disqualification. [Report guidelines, section 9, pp. 4-5](https://www.planet-simtech.de/wp-content/uploads/2023/05/PlaNeT_Vorgaben-Bericht.pdf#page=4)
- For low-level stylistic assistance, the guidelines give an example of one sentence in the introduction naming the tools and functions used for final language polishing. Output still has to be read and checked, especially where a suggested synonym changes scientific meaning. [Report guidelines, section 10, p. 5](https://www.planet-simtech.de/wp-content/uploads/2023/05/PlaNeT_Vorgaben-Bericht.pdf#page=5)
- Higher-level rewriting such as an "AI Humanizer" is described as questionable. It should be documented and justified section by section, for example in a footnote at the start of the affected section. [Report guidelines, section 10, p. 5](https://www.planet-simtech.de/wp-content/uploads/2023/05/PlaNeT_Vorgaben-Bericht.pdf#page=5)
- For AI-generated illustrations, diagrams, or images, name the tool and state how extensively the team edited the result. The tool will typically be named in the figure caption. The exact prompt is not required. [Report guidelines, section 10, pp. 5-6](https://www.planet-simtech.de/wp-content/uploads/2023/05/PlaNeT_Vorgaben-Bericht.pdf#page=6)
- For AI-generated code, make the degree of use explicit. The official examples distinguish using a tool only for debugging from generating a first draft that the team later refined. [Report guidelines, section 10, p. 6](https://www.planet-simtech.de/wp-content/uploads/2023/05/PlaNeT_Vorgaben-Bericht.pdf#page=6)
- Exact prompts are also not required for LLM queries. Using an LLM for initial ideas is allowed, but its factual output still requires checking and source support. [Report guidelines, section 10, p. 6](https://www.planet-simtech.de/wp-content/uploads/2023/05/PlaNeT_Vorgaben-Bericht.pdf#page=6)

### Inferences

- Treat the page budget as a production constraint from the first draft. Keep every claim, derivation, result, and source needed to understand the argument inside the 10-page main report. Put only nonessential code in the appendix, because the jury need not read it and separate files are not accepted. [Report guidelines, sections 1 and 2, p. 2](https://www.planet-simtech.de/wp-content/uploads/2023/05/PlaNeT_Vorgaben-Bericht.pdf#page=2); [Official FAQ](https://www.planet-simtech.de/faq/)
- Use one cover page, even though the rules do not state a separate numerical cover-page limit. Include only the required identifiers and task information, not a D4RT-style author or affiliation block. [Report guidelines, section 1, p. 1](https://www.planet-simtech.de/wp-content/uploads/2023/05/PlaNeT_Vorgaben-Bericht.pdf#page=1)
- Extend the anonymity check to headers, footers, acknowledgements, figure credits, code comments, and visible file properties. The official text explicitly covers the cover and report, while cleaning PDF metadata is a prudent implementation measure rather than a separately stated rule. [Participation conditions, section 10, pp. 2-3](https://www.planet-simtech.de/wp-content/uploads/2023/05/PlaNeT_Teilnahmebedingungen.pdf#page=3)
- Apply 12 pt to body text, captions, table text, footnotes, and bibliography for a strict compliance check. The rule states 12 pt without publishing exceptions for those elements. Do not copy D4RT's smaller sizes unless the organizer explicitly confirms that they are allowed. [Report guidelines, section 2, p. 2](https://www.planet-simtech.de/wp-content/uploads/2023/05/PlaNeT_Vorgaben-Bericht.pdf#page=2)
- Keep an AI-use log during the work. Record the system, task, affected section or asset, human checks, and degree of later editing. This makes the required contextual disclosures reproducible under time pressure. Exact prompts can stay out of the report because the guidelines do not require them. [Report guidelines, sections 9 and 10, pp. 4-6](https://www.planet-simtech.de/wp-content/uploads/2023/05/PlaNeT_Vorgaben-Bericht.pdf#page=4)
- Plan to email `Team-ID.pdf` before 16:00 Stuttgart time, obtain the confirmation email, and check the captain's pre-event and same-day messages for changed instructions. A local timestamp of 16:00 is not a safe target for the actual send operation. [Official FAQ](https://www.planet-simtech.de/faq/); [Participation conditions, section 11, p. 3](https://www.planet-simtech.de/wp-content/uploads/2023/05/PlaNeT_Teilnahmebedingungen.pdf#page=3)

### Gaps

- The published rules do not say whether a report may use two columns, or whether captions, tables, footnotes, and references may be smaller than 12 pt. They also do not prescribe a caption position, heading hierarchy, citation backend, or LaTeX class. [Report guidelines, section 2, p. 2](https://www.planet-simtech.de/wp-content/uploads/2023/05/PlaNeT_Vorgaben-Bericht.pdf#page=2); [Official FAQ](https://www.planet-simtech.de/faq/)
- No official LaTeX or word-processing template is supplied. [Official FAQ, "Gibt es eine Vorlage für den Bericht?"](https://www.planet-simtech.de/faq/)
- The rules give disclosure examples but no single mandatory AI-declaration template or one mandatory location for every kind of use. Placement depends on the use, such as the introduction for global language polishing, a caption for an image, or a section footnote for higher-level rewriting. [Report guidelines, section 10, pp. 5-6](https://www.planet-simtech.de/wp-content/uploads/2023/05/PlaNeT_Vorgaben-Bericht.pdf#page=5)
- The exact operative submission time and any changed submission channel are organizer communications. The public FAQ's 16:00 time is the normal published deadline, not permission to ignore a later email. [Official FAQ](https://www.planet-simtech.de/faq/); [Participation conditions, sections 10 and 11, pp. 2-3](https://www.planet-simtech.de/wp-content/uploads/2023/05/PlaNeT_Teilnahmebedingungen.pdf#page=3)

## 2. What the current site says about the final video

### Takeaway

Finalists present their PlaNeT Day work as an online Science Slam with a video of at most 5:00 minutes, publicly broadcast during the live final. The organizer supplies the video format, resolution, and other detailed criteria to finalists later. [Official homepage](https://www.planet-simtech.de/); [Participation conditions, section 15, p. 3](https://www.planet-simtech.de/wp-content/uploads/2023/05/PlaNeT_Teilnahmebedingungen.pdf#page=3); [Official FAQ](https://www.planet-simtech.de/faq/)

### Cited Findings

- The homepage sets the current final date to 27.02.2027. It describes an online Science Slam in which teams present themselves and their PlaNeT Day solutions to an online audience. [Official homepage](https://www.planet-simtech.de/)
- The video may not be longer than five minutes. This is a maximum, not a requirement to fill exactly five minutes. The homepage encourages an entertaining contribution. No minimum duration is stated. [Official homepage](https://www.planet-simtech.de/)
- Selected teams create a video according to criteria communicated in advance. The participation conditions name duration and resolution as examples of those criteria, and state that videos will be publicly broadcast during the final. [Participation conditions, section 15, p. 3](https://www.planet-simtech.de/wp-content/uploads/2023/05/PlaNeT_Teilnahmebedingungen.pdf#page=3)

- Consent to photo, audio, and video processing is voluntary, and refusal does not exclude a participant from the competition. Participants can decide whether recordings of them are created for the video submission and live event. Consent paperwork is supplied to finalists and, for minors, to their legal representatives. [Participation conditions, sections 5 and 6, pp. 1-2](https://www.planet-simtech.de/wp-content/uploads/2023/05/PlaNeT_Teilnahmebedingungen.pdf#page=2)

[Participation conditions, section 18.1, p. 4](https://www.planet-simtech.de/wp-content/uploads/2023/05/PlaNeT_Teilnahmebedingungen.pdf#page=4)

The FAQ says a voting URL will be published during the final and the team with the most votes wins, while the conditions give the jury the tie decision. [Participation conditions, section 19.3, p. 4](https://www.planet-simtech.de/wp-content/uploads/2023/05/PlaNeT_Teilnahmebedingungen.pdf#page=4); [Official FAQ, "Fragen zum Finale"](https://www.planet-simtech.de/faq/); [Official homepage](https://www.planet-simtech.de/)

## 3. What D4RT offers as a layout benchmark

### Takeaway

D4RT gets its craft from a consistent Times-style hierarchy, disciplined 1.2 body leading, compact two-column geometry, explanatory captions, and tables built from horizontal rules rather than boxes. Its 10 pt body, 9 pt captions and references, 7 pt table text, US Letter paper, and roughly 20.7 mm side margins conflict with a strict reading of the PlaNeT rules. Reproduce the hierarchy and alignment, not those measurements. [D4RT paper, pp. 1-2 and 6](https://storage.googleapis.com/d4rt_assets/D4RT_paper.pdf#page=1); [Report guidelines, section 2, p. 2](https://www.planet-simtech.de/wp-content/uploads/2023/05/PlaNeT_Vorgaben-Bericht.pdf#page=2)

### Cited Findings

#### Page and text geometry

- The D4RT PDF has 17 US Letter pages, 612 x 792 pt, and was produced with pdfTeX. US Letter is 215.9 x 279.4 mm, so it is not the required A4 format. [D4RT paper, document properties](https://storage.googleapis.com/d4rt_assets/D4RT_paper.pdf); [Report guidelines, section 2, p. 2](https://www.planet-simtech.de/wp-content/uploads/2023/05/PlaNeT_Vorgaben-Bericht.pdf#page=2)
- The main text uses embedded NimbusRomNo9L regular, medium, and italic fonts, with Computer Modern math fonts. NimbusRomNo9L is the Times-style text face visible in the paper. [D4RT paper, pp. 1-2 and embedded font list](https://storage.googleapis.com/d4rt_assets/D4RT_paper.pdf#page=1)
- Ordinary body text measures 10 pt with 12 pt baseline spacing. The first-line paragraph indent measures about 11.7 pt, and new paragraphs do not receive a large blank gap. [D4RT paper, p. 1](https://storage.googleapis.com/d4rt_assets/D4RT_paper.pdf#page=1)
- The two main columns begin at approximately x = 58.7 pt and x = 317.3 pt. Ordinary lines are about 236.3 pt wide, which is 83.4 mm. ### Inferences

#### Rule-safe LaTeX specification

- Use A4 with `margin=25mm`. This yields a 160 x 247 mm text block. Do not reproduce D4RT's US Letter paper or 20.7 mm side margins. [D4RT paper, p. 1](https://storage.googleapis.com/d4rt_assets/D4RT_paper.pdf#page=1); [Report guidelines, section 2, p. 2](https://www.planet-simtech.de/wp-content/uploads/2023/05/PlaNeT_Vorgaben-Bericht.pdf#page=2)
- A two-column layout is a reasonable adaptation, not an official requirement. With the required 160 mm text width and an 8 mm column separation, each column is 76 mm wide. This is close to D4RT's 7.8 mm gutter but narrower than its 83.4 mm columns. Check German hyphenation and overfull lines carefully. Switch to one column if the result becomes hard to read rather than reducing type or margins. [D4RT paper, pp. 1-2](https://storage.googleapis.com/d4rt_assets/D4RT_paper.pdf#page=2); [Report guidelines, section 2, p. 2](https://www.planet-simtech.de/wp-content/uploads/2023/05/PlaNeT_Vorgaben-Bericht.pdf#page=2)
- Use a Times-compatible text and math setup, such as `newtxtext` with `newtxmath` in a pdfLaTeX workflow, or TeX Gyre Termes and Termes Math in a Unicode workflow. These are implementation recommendations based on the permitted "comparable" font clause and D4RT's Nimbus Roman appearance, not claims about packages already installed in the report environment. [Report guidelines, section 2, p. 2](https://www.planet-simtech.de/wp-content/uploads/2023/05/PlaNeT_Vorgaben-Bericht.pdf#page=2); [D4RT paper, embedded fonts](https://storage.googleapis.com/d4rt_assets/D4RT_paper.pdf#page=1)
- Set body text to 12 pt on approximately 14.4 to 14.5 pt leading. This preserves D4RT's measured 1.2 font-to-leading ratio and exceeds the official minimum line spacing of 1.0. Use German language hyphenation, justified paragraphs, a modest first-line indent, and no large paragraph gaps. [D4RT paper, p. 1](https://storage.googleapis.com/d4rt_assets/D4RT_paper.pdf#page=1); [Report guidelines, section 2, p. 2](https://www.planet-simtech.de/wp-content/uploads/2023/05/PlaNeT_Vorgaben-Bericht.pdf#page=2)
- Scale the heading hierarchy rather than copying its absolute sizes. A practical strict set is a 17 pt bold centred report title, 14 pt bold left-aligned main sections, 12 pt bold subsections, and a 14 pt bold centred summary heading. The 17 pt title approximates D4RT's title-to-body ratio of 14.3 to 10, scaled to a 12 pt body. [D4RT paper, p. 1](https://storage.googleapis.com/d4rt_assets/D4RT_paper.pdf#page=1)
- Keep captions, table text, footnotes, and references at 12 pt for the conservative compliance version. D4RT's 9 pt captions, 9 pt references, and 7 pt large-table text are useful evidence of hierarchy but are not safe values for this competition. Split dense tables or reduce their column count instead of shrinking type. [D4RT paper, pp. 6 and 9](https://storage.googleapis.com/d4rt_assets/D4RT_paper.pdf#page=6); [Report guidelines, section 2, p. 2](https://www.planet-simtech.de/wp-content/uploads/2023/05/PlaNeT_Vorgaben-Bericht.pdf#page=2)
- Use a separate cover with the competition name, task statement, date, and Team-ID. Do not imitate D4RT's author names, affiliations, correspondence footnotes, or project URL block. The summary begins after the cover, remains within the 10-page budget, and uses plain German rather than D4RT's technical abstract style. [D4RT paper, p. 1](https://storage.googleapis.com/d4rt_assets/D4RT_paper.pdf#page=1); [Report guidelines, section 1, p. 1](https://www.planet-simtech.de/wp-content/uploads/2023/05/PlaNeT_Vorgaben-Bericht.pdf#page=1)

#### Figure, table, and citation treatment

- Put simple diagrams in one column and complex schematics or multi-panel result figures across both columns. Keep captions below figures for visual consistency with D4RT. A caption should state the numbered label, the result or comparison, and the reading needed to understand the panel. Add the source credit and any required AI-tool disclosure to that same caption. [D4RT paper, pp. 1-2 and 6](https://storage.googleapis.com/d4rt_assets/D4RT_paper.pdf#page=6); [Participation conditions, section 14, p. 3](https://www.planet-simtech.de/wp-content/uploads/2023/05/PlaNeT_Teilnahmebedingungen.pdf#page=3); [Report guidelines, section 10, pp. 5-6](https://www.planet-simtech.de/wp-content/uploads/2023/05/PlaNeT_Vorgaben-Bericht.pdf#page=6)
- D4RT places the inspected table captions below their tables, but PlaNeT publishes no caption-position rule. Consistency matters more than copying that choice. If following D4RT literally, place table captions below as well; otherwise use the more common above-table convention consistently. [D4RT paper, Tables 1, 3, and 4](https://storage.googleapis.com/d4rt_assets/D4RT_paper.pdf#page=6); [Report guidelines, section 2, p. 2](https://www.planet-simtech.de/wp-content/uploads/2023/05/PlaNeT_Vorgaben-Bericht.pdf#page=2)
- Recreate D4RT's table discipline with horizontal rules only, grouped headers, aligned decimal columns, consistent precision, units and direction-of-better markers in headers, and dashes for unavailable values. Use one light accent fill for selected or best cells and bold row labels sparingly. Avoid vertical rules, heavy boxes, and several competing highlight colours. [D4RT paper, Table 4, p. 6](https://storage.googleapis.com/d4rt_assets/D4RT_paper.pdf#page=6)
- Make wide result tables span both columns before reducing font size. If 12 pt text still does not fit, split the table by model family or result group and preserve the same headers. D4RT's 7 pt density is not a lawful target under a strict reading of the rules. [D4RT paper, Table 4, p. 6](https://storage.googleapis.com/d4rt_assets/D4RT_paper.pdf#page=6); [Report guidelines, section 2, p. 2](https://www.planet-simtech.de/wp-content/uploads/2023/05/PlaNeT_Vorgaben-Bericht.pdf#page=2)
- Use bracketed numeric citations directly after the supported claim. D4RT supports the visual convention, while the official report guidelines control the bibliography contents and web-access dates. Alphabetical numbering is a valid design option seen in D4RT, not an official PlaNeT requirement. [D4RT paper, pp. 2 and 9](https://storage.googleapis.com/d4rt_assets/D4RT_paper.pdf#page=9); [Report guidelines, section 3, pp. 2-3](https://www.planet-simtech.de/wp-content/uploads/2023/05/PlaNeT_Vorgaben-Bericht.pdf#page=2)
- Keep headings and body text black. Reserve a single restrained accent for links, selected table cells, and semantically meaningful figure series. D4RT demonstrates this restrained use of blue; it does not justify decorative colour bars or coloured section titles. [D4RT paper, pp. 1 and 6](https://storage.googleapis.com/d4rt_assets/D4RT_paper.pdf#page=6)
- D4RT's abstract is italic, but italic is optional for the PlaNeT summary. Upright 12 pt type is the clearer default for a full jargon-free summary of up to one page. Retain D4RT's centred bold heading and compact spacing instead. [D4RT paper, p. 1](https://storage.googleapis.com/d4rt_assets/D4RT_paper.pdf#page=1); [Report guidelines, section 1, p. 1](https://www.planet-simtech.de/wp-content/uploads/2023/05/PlaNeT_Vorgaben-Bericht.pdf#page=1)

### Gaps

- Only the D4RT PDF was supplied. Without its LaTeX source, exact class names, package options, and nominal source-font declarations cannot be confirmed. The measurements above come from PDF text objects and may differ by a few hundredths of a point from source values. [D4RT paper](https://storage.googleapis.com/d4rt_assets/D4RT_paper.pdf)
- The PDF alone does not establish which D4RT figures were vector or raster before export, or which plotting program produced them. It does support measurements of final text, column positions, captions, and visible table structure. [D4RT paper](https://storage.googleapis.com/d4rt_assets/D4RT_paper.pdf)
- The official rules do not resolve whether D4RT-style two columns are preferred, whether smaller non-body text is acceptable, or whether table captions should be above or below. The strict 12 pt recommendation is therefore a compliance choice, not a quotation of a more detailed official rule. [Report guidelines, section 2, p. 2](https://www.planet-simtech.de/wp-content/uploads/2023/05/PlaNeT_Vorgaben-Bericht.pdf#page=2)
- No Blautopf scientific, geographic, or thermal-performance evidence was researched here. D4RT supplies no evidence for those subjects and must remain a layout-only citation. [D4RT paper](https://storage.googleapis.com/d4rt_assets/D4RT_paper.pdf)
